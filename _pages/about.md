**Dr. Andrés A. Zúñiga** is an Assistant Professor at the Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa ([ISEL](https://www.isel.pt/)) and a collaborating member of the Institute of Mechanical Engineering ([IDMEC](https://www.idmec.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/)), an R&D unit within LAETA – the Associated Laboratory of Energy, Transports and Aerospace.

---

### Research & Interests

His research focuses on the **reliability, availability, maintainability, and safety (RAMS)** of engineering systems, with particular interest in electrical, robotic, and industrial automation systems. His main research interests include:

* Risk analysis & modeling of failures and degradation processes
* Condition-based and predictive maintenance
* Fault diagnosis and prognosis
* Application of artificial intelligence techniques and hybrid approaches to system condition assessment and service life

### Teaching & Academic Activity

At ISEL, he teaches in the areas of **robotics, automation, and instrumentation**. 

Previously, he was a member of the teaching staff of the Master's Programme in Reliability and Maintenance Engineering (MICRO) at the Fundación Universitaria de Las Palmas, teaching physical asset management and risk analysis. He was also an Invited Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico. Throughout his academic career, he has supervised and co-supervised dissertations and research work in the areas of reliability, maintenance, risk, and engineering systems.

He is actively involved in specialized training and knowledge transfer in RAMS engineering, having delivered specialized training courses for professionals from Angola's Ministry of Energy and Water. He also maintains an academic collaboration with the Department of Electrical, Electronic and Telecommunications Engineering at the University of Cuenca (Ecuador), providing specialized training in RAMS engineering applied to electrical and electronic systems.
